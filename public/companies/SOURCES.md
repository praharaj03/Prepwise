# Logo sources

NVIDIA, Zomato, SpaceX, Swiggy, Apple, and Netflix SVGs: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under CC0. Google and Oracle SVGs: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), distributed under MIT. Microsoft’s four-square mark is represented with SVG rectangles. Amazon uses the existing project image from `public/covers/amazon.png`.

Company names and logos identify independent practice samples, not official interview questions or a partnership with the companies.
